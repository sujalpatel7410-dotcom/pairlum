    Your memories stay yours. A private relationship is not an advertising product.

    Pairlum is built around private relationships, memories and experiences shared between two people. Privacy is part of the product, not an optional add-on. This Policy explains what personal data Pairlum collects, why it is used, how it may be shared, how long it may be kept, and the choices and rights available to you.

      1Scope of this Privacy Policy

      This Policy applies to pairlum.com, Pairlum accounts and Couple Spaces, Memories, Letters and Open When experiences, Chapters, Time Capsules, Parallel Moments, reactions and partner activity, Pairlum Book creation and fulfilment, free public tools, customer support, payment and membership administration, and other Pairlum features that link to this Policy.

      Third-party services that Pairlum does not control have their own privacy policies, and those policies apply to them.

      In this Policy, "Pairlum", "we", "us" and "our" mean [Legal name of the entity operating Pairlum], [Legal / business address], India.

      2Pairlum is for adults

      Pairlum V1 is intended only for people aged 18 or older. By creating an account you confirm that you are at least 18. If Pairlum learns that an ineligible minor has created an account, Pairlum may restrict or delete that account and its data as required by law.

      3The privacy principle behind Pairlum

      Pairlum is designed around these principles:

      • A private relationship is not a public social network.
      • Private memories are private by default.
      • You keep ownership of the content you create.
      • Pairlum does not sell private relationship content or intimate relationship information to advertisers.
      • Private memories are not used to train AI models by default.
      • Only data reasonably necessary to operate and improve the service should be collected.
      • One partner should not automatically be able to erase everything belonging to the other.
      • Marketing use of private Couple content requires separate permission.
      • You should be able to understand, access, correct, export and delete eligible data, subject to law and to the rights of the other person in your Couple Space.

      4Information we collect — account information

      When you create or manage a Pairlum account, Pairlum may collect your name, email address, authentication and account identifiers, your 18+ confirmation, an optional profile image, your account settings, security, login and session information, and the dates your account was created or changed.

      Government ID should not be routinely required, unless it is needed for a specific legal, security or fraud reason.

      5Couple Space and relationship information

      You may choose to provide your partner's name or email so an invitation can be sent, a relationship start or anniversary date, a Couple Space name, an optional cover image, Chapters, milestones, long-distance status where relevant, and other relationship information you add voluntarily.

      Pairlum should not require unnecessary sensitive details simply to use the service.

      6Private Content you create

      Pairlum may process photos, videos, voice recordings, notes, captions, Memories, Letters, Open When and other locked content, Time Capsules, Chapters, reactions, Parallel Moments, reunion experiences, dates and locations you add, Book selections and layouts, and other relationship content you intentionally add. This Policy calls all of it Private Content.

      Private Content is not public by default.

      7Information your partner may provide about you

      A connected partner may upload content that relates to you, invite you, write you a Letter, create shared Memories or contribute to shared objects. Once you join a Couple Space, information may be available to the other partner according to the rules of each feature.

      Joining Pairlum does not give Pairlum advertising rights over that content.

      8Device, technical and usage information

      Pairlum may receive limited browser, device, operating system, IP, network, session, app version, referral, error, feature event, upload, performance and security information.

      Analytics should measure whether features work, rather than the substance of a relationship. For example, Pairlum may record that a memory was created without sending the private photograph or caption to analytics.

      9Location and photo metadata

      Media files can contain capture dates, device information and GPS metadata. Pairlum may extract useful metadata to organise a Memory, remove unnecessary metadata from optimised viewing copies to reduce accidental disclosure, and preserve the private original where appropriate.

      You should be able to control whether a location is visibly attached to a Memory.

      10Payment information

      Payments may be processed by an external payment provider. Pairlum may keep the order and payment ID, amount, currency, status, date, cohort and refund state, together with the limited method information the processor gives us.

      Pairlum does not intend to store full card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

      11Pairlum Book information

      When an eligible Couple chooses to create a Book, Pairlum may process Book eligibility, the memories and media you select, layout and cover choices, proof approval, print-quality status, the recipient, shipping and phone details needed for delivery, and courier, production and delivery information.

      A shipping address should be collected when it is needed for fulfilment, not unnecessarily at signup.

      12Customer support and feedback

      Support and feedback may include your contact information, support tickets, screenshots you choose to send, technical details and our communications with you. Founding Couples may also volunteer product feedback.

      Giving feedback does not transfer ownership of your Private Content to Pairlum.

      13Why Pairlum uses personal data

      Pairlum may process personal data to:

      • Provide Pairlum and the features you ask for.
      • Protect accounts, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain the integrity of the system.
      • Operate payments, refunds, membership, and Book eligibility and fulfilment.
      • Send verification, invitation, reset, security, payment, service and Book messages.
      • Improve onboarding, reliability, feature adoption and performance.
      • Meet valid legal, accounting, security and dispute obligations.

      The lawful basis that applies depends on the circumstances and on applicable law.

      14Your content remains yours

      You keep ownership of the content you create and upload, subject to any rights other people independently hold. Pairlum receives only a limited, non-exclusive permission to host, store, back up, technically reproduce, create thumbnails and previews, transcode, display to authorised users, transmit, generate approved Book layouts, and reproduce approved content in a Book, as necessary to provide the service.

      This is not a general right to sell your memories or to advertise with them.

      15AI and your private memories

      Pairlum may offer optional AI-assisted features such as organisation, Book layout suggestions, duplicate detection or question generation.

      Your private photos, videos, voice recordings, letters, notes, capsules and relationship content are not used as a general AI training dataset by default. If a feature you ask for sends Private Content to an AI service, Pairlum should explain that processing. Any future use for model training that goes beyond providing a feature you requested requires a separate, appropriate permission process.

      16Pairlum does not sell your private relationship

      Pairlum does not sell Private Content or intimate relationship information to advertisers, and does not intend to build advertising profiles based on private letters, photos, conflicts, inferred relationship quality or similarly intimate information.

      Pairlum's business model is intended to be based on products and services provided to couples.

      17When we share information

      Private Content is not disclosed publicly by default. Pairlum may share information:

      • With your connected partner, according to the rules of each feature.
      • With service providers that are necessary to operate the product.
      • Where a valid legal or safety obligation applies.
      • In a business reorganisation, subject to law and to appropriate protections.
      • With your separate permission, for testimonials or marketing.

      Founding status on its own is not marketing consent.

      18Public profiles and social discovery

      Pairlum V1 is not designed as a public social network. By default it does not provide public Couple profiles, public memory feeds, public follower lists, publicly searchable couples or relationship-popularity scores.

      If an optional sharing feature is added in future, it should clearly identify the intended audience before anything is shared.

      19Storage and international processing

      Pairlum and its service providers may process and store information in India or in other countries, using distributed cloud infrastructure. Cross-border processing should be handled in accordance with applicable Indian law and with any restrictions or safeguards that apply.

      20How we protect information

      Pairlum uses safeguards designed to protect information. These may include authenticated access, authorisation checks, private object storage, short-lived signed media access, encryption in transit and at rest where supported, Row Level Security, multi-factor authentication for administrators, limited permissions, audit logging, monitoring, backups and restricted production access.

      No service can promise 100% security, and Pairlum does not claim end-to-end encryption unless it has actually been implemented and communicated.

      21Pairlum administrator access

      Ordinary administration should not involve casually browsing Couple content. Admin tools should mainly expose account, membership, payment, storage, upload, Book and support metadata.

      Authorised access to Private Content for a legitimate support, safety, security or legal reason should be restricted, limited to that purpose, and audited wherever this is technically practical.

      22How long we retain information

      Pairlum keeps personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, and for legal obligations, security and disputes.

      • Data for an active account may be kept while it is needed to operate the service.
      • Eligible deleted content is intended to spend 30 days in Recently Deleted before permanent-deletion processing.
      • Backups may retain deleted data temporarily, until those backups expire.
      • Payment and legal records, and Book production records, may require longer lawful retention.

      Exact production retention schedules: [To be finalised and published]

      23Deleting a Couple Space is different from deleting an individual account

      Pairlum treats an individual account and a shared Couple Space as two different things. Deleting one person's account does not automatically mean that the whole shared Couple Space, or all of the content belonging to the other partner, is erased.

      What is deleted depends on who created or contributed the content, whether it is shared, the rights of the other user, legal obligations and backup processes. The product should clearly explain the consequences before any destructive action.

      24Separation or leaving a Couple Space

      Pairlum recognises that relationships can change or end. Where the feature is available, you may leave a Couple Space, export eligible data, delete your individual account or remove eligible content, and ask support for help with shared-data issues.

      Pairlum should not use streaks, guilt notifications or manipulative mechanics to pressure anyone into staying in a relationship or a Couple Space.

      25Your privacy choices and rights

      Subject to applicable law and to other people's rights, you may:

      • Ask for access to, and information about, your personal data.
      • Correct information that is inaccurate.
      • Delete eligible data or your account.
      • Withdraw consent where consent applies.
      • Use or request the export feature.
      • Raise a grievance.
      • Exercise nomination rights where and when they apply.

      Requests may need secure identity verification, and may be limited where the law or another person's rights require it.

      26How to make a privacy request

      Write to privacy@pairlum.com, or use the controls available in your account.

      Please include enough information for us to identify the account and understand the request. Pairlum may verify your identity before acting, and will respond within the timelines required by applicable law.

      28Cookies and similar technologies

      Pairlum may use cookies, local storage, and session, authentication and analytics technologies. Essential technologies support authentication, security, sessions and preferences. Analytics may measure website and product usage and reliability. Marketing technologies may be added later.

      Where consent or other legal requirements apply, Pairlum should provide appropriate controls for non-essential tracking, and must not label advertising trackers as essential. Full detail is in the Cookie & Tracking Notice.

      29Free public tools

      Free tools such as Couple Questions, Date Ideas and Love Messages may process the inputs needed to generate a result. Pairlum should avoid keeping unnecessarily intimate inputs where long-term storage is not needed.

      If you choose to save a result into Pairlum, it may then become part of your Pairlum data.

      30Email communications

      Pairlum may send essential verification, invitation, security, reset, payment, membership, capsule, letter and Book messages.

      Marketing messages should offer an appropriate way to opt out. Opting out of marketing does not stop necessary transactional and security messages.

      31Security incidents and data breaches

      Pairlum maintains an internal process to investigate, contain and document suspected security incidents, work out which systems and data are affected, reduce ongoing risk, and provide the authority and user notifications required under applicable law.
